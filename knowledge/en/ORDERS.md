# ORDERS · Real positions, adoption and execution
- Monitoring is the default. Orders require a subaccount key and explicit Mainnet enablement. On activation the app reads the selected market under the account lock and automatically adopts the full position and direction reported by Lighter, including manual positions. Activation itself sends no order and accepts future signals only.
- Active market orders, pending execution in its journal or a flat market with a mismatched journal block adoption. A position in another market prevents arming on a flat market and blocks new entries on that account.
- The first new turn against the position closes the real size with a `reduce_only` market order. A partial fill can trigger attempts to close the remainder. The app requires verified flat status before opening the other side and pauses if execution cannot be reconciled.
- The configured margin is the base. After a reconciled win, the target margin is the used margin plus profit, floored at the base. A loss resets the target to the base. Entries use the lesser of the target and available balance, subject to market minimums and the 20× cap. The journal preserves margin across sessions. Order bounds use book depth. Insufficient liquidity can prevent a full close.
- Reinvestment uses the change in available balance between the flat account before entry and the flat account after exit. Use a dedicated USDC subaccount with no deposits, withdrawals or other trades while a position is open, as those flows affect the measurement. Adopted positions without a reference balance close normally and start the next cycle at base margin.
- Configure the account drawdown trigger in Settings → Orders. It defaults to 25%. An empty field or zero disables it (`--drawdown 0` in the terminal). EMA exits remain active. When enabled, each refresh compares Lighter's total account value with the stored peak by network and account. A breach attempts to close the managed position with `reduce_only` and pauses entries. After closing, explicit reactivation starts a new peak. Losses can exceed the trigger between updates or when liquidity is limited.
- Switching charts preserves the armed market. With a position there, the window returns on the next refresh after a five-minute browsing window. An optional beep marks a confirmed action.
- Code `Monitor.arm`, `broker.execute`, `broker.connect`. [RECONNECT](RECONNECT.md) · [DATA](DATA.md)
- If Lighter confirms a slippage cancellation with zero fill, the app rechecks the position and order book and retries once immediately. If the same cancellation recurs, it retries on later reads at least two minutes apart while the signal remains valid. A partial fill, uncertain outcome or another cancellation reason pauses automation.
- Remove `--adopt-size` from older commands. The app now reads the position automatically for adoption.
